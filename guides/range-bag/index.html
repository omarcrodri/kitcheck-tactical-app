Range bag

A range bag holds what you need for a safe session at the bay. This page does not link out to buy a firearm or ammunition. Those buy links, when the app shows them, open another site and stay behind the age check.

Eyes and ears

  • Eye protection the range acceptsThe line throws brass, fragments, and the unexpected. Bring a pair you will actually wear.
  • Ear protection, plus a spareRanges are loud enough to damage hearing. Muffs, plugs, or both, and a second pair if the first gets lost.

The firearm and ammunition

  • The firearm you will shootKeep it unloaded until the range rules say otherwise, and cased if the range requires a case.
  • Ammunition that matches that firearmRead the barrel, the manual, and the box. The app’s fit checks are a guide. The markings on the gun decide.
  • Magazines that fit, and a way to set them on the benchA magazine that does not lock in is not a spare. Confirm the fit before you leave home.

The bay

  • Targets, tape or a stapler, and a markerSkip these if your range provides them. Bring them if it does not.
  • A chamber flag if the range uses one, and a small toolA cold range means hands off. Know the rule before you open the case.
  • Water, a hat, and sun protection for an outdoor bayA long string of fire is hotter and longer than it feels at the car.
  • Your identification and the range rulesMany ranges ask to see an ID, and their rules override a general list.

On the line

  • Treat every firearm as if it is loaded.That includes one you just cleared.
  • Keep the muzzle in a safe direction the range allows.A safe direction is the berm or the backstop, not the people beside you.
  • Keep your finger outside the trigger guard until you are ready to fire.Ready means the sights are on the target and the range is hot.
  • Know the target and what is beyond it.A miss still goes somewhere.
