72-hour bag

A 72-hour bag is a pack you can carry if you need to leave home, or stay out, for about three days. Pack for the people who would carry it and for the weather where you live. Swap anything that does not fit your household.

Water

  • Drinking waterPlan on roughly a gallon per person per day, and more in heat. Carry some of it, and leave room to refill.
  • A way to treat more waterA small filter, tablets, or a pot you can boil in. The water you start with runs out.

Food

  • Ready-to-eat food for three daysChoose food you can eat if you cannot cook. A stove is useful and is one more thing to fuel.
  • A way to open and eat itA simple utensil and a bag for trash. Hunger is easier to manage when the meal is not a project.

Clothing and shelter

  • A layer, a rain shell, and a hatWeather stops people before the food does.
  • Gloves and shoes you can walk inBlisters and cold hands end a walk early.
  • A compact emergency blanket or a small tarpA dry place to sit matters at night.

Light, power, and paper

  • A headlamp and spare powerHands stay free after dark. Bring spare batteries or a way to recharge.
  • A paper map and a short list of phone numbersA dead phone is not a route, and cell service is not a plan.
  • Copies of identification and a little cashSmall bills are easier to use when a card reader is down.

Health and a few tools

  • Medicines you already takeBring at least three days, in a bag you can identify by touch.
  • Bandages, blister care, and hygiene itemsSmall problems are a common reason people stop.
  • A multi-tool, some cord, and a whistleA whistle carries farther than a voice when you need to be found.

Water, food, and a paper map are not slots in the builder. The button fills the slots the app does have.

Build it in the app

The button opens the builder and fills empty slots from this list. A slot you already filled stays as it is. These are catalog records you can swap.
