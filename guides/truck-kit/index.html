Truck kit

A truck kit stays with the vehicle: weather, a breakdown, and the walk if you have to leave the vehicle. A parked truck is a poor place to leave a firearm. Follow the law where you park, and keep dangerous items out of reach.

If you have to walk

  • A pack you can carryIf the truck stops, the kit has to leave with you. The truck starter fills an empty pack slot with a truck pack from the catalog.
  • Boots, work gloves, and eye protectionRoadside work and a walk both need them. The starter fills these slots when they are empty.
  • A shell layer and a warm hatThe cab is shelter only while you are in it. The starter uses a shell and a beanie from the catalog, not a helmet.

Weather and the vehicle

  • A blanket or something warmWaiting for a tow in the cold is the job this item does.
  • Drinking water and a shelf-stable snackHeat and cold in a cab ruin food. Rotate what you store there.
  • A flashlight and a way to be seenA bright cloth or a triangle helps other drivers see a stopped vehicle.
  • A way to deal with a flat or a dead batteryA tire tool, or jumper cables or a jump pack, and practice using them before you need them.

Paper and a small kit

  • A map, a pen, and a little cashUseful when the phone is dead or the card reader is down.
  • A copy of the insurance card and a phone cableKeep the copy where you can reach it without unpacking the whole truck.
  • A small first-aid kit and personal medicinesCuts and a missed dose are the usual problems, not a dramatic one.

The truck starter does not add a plate carrier, a chest rig, or soft armor. The catalog has no first-aid kit, handheld light, water container, tool, or recovery item, so the button cannot fill those. Pack them yourself.

Build it in the app

The button opens the builder and fills empty slots from this list. A slot you already filled stays as it is. These are catalog records you can swap.
