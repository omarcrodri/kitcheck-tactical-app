KitCheck Tactical

About

KitCheck Tactical is a loadout builder that runs in your browser. You lay out a kit, mark what you already own, and see which slots are still open.

Setup asks you to choose Civilian shooter, Prepper, or Military. A military profile can also mark gear as issued. The kit, the profile, and the photos you add stay on this device.

How we earn

We may earn a commission from some links at no extra cost to you. Store screens in the app say: We may earn a commission when you buy through links.

The price file shipped with the app is labeled example data. Its links point at example.com. They are not a live store, and the app does not add a fee of its own.

Firearms and ammunition

The app does not sell firearms or ammunition. A buy link or a store page opens another site. For a gun, ammunition, a frame, or a receiver, that link stays hidden until the age saved on this device meets the dealer minimum the app uses for your state.

Handguns and frames use the handgun minimum. Rifles, shotguns, and ammunition use the long-gun minimum. If age is missing, those links stay hidden. The app shows this line for those items: Guns ship to a licensed dealer (FFL). Check your state law. It also says the age line is an estimate, not legal advice.

Some ammunition records can also show a compare link to ammoseek.com. That link uses the same age check as the other ammunition links, the long-gun minimum for your state. It opens in a new tab when you choose it.

Contact

Questions go to support@kitchecktactical.com. The support form in the app opens your email app with the kind of note, the app version, the current loadout id, a reply address if you type one, and your message. Nothing is sent until you send that email.
